# Brand assets

Real Safari Tours Sharm logo supplied by the owner (2026-10-05), with the
bottom contact line (website and phone) cropped out at his request and the
white background made transparent. Source: `~/Downloads/logo.jpg`.

- `logo-full.webp` — the full stacked logo (footer, about, documents).
- `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-512.png`, `../favicon.svg` — the emblem.
- `../og-default.png` — social share image.

The same artwork works on light and dark backgrounds; `BrandLogo.vue` uses it
for both themes.
