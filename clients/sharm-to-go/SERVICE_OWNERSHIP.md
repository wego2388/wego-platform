# Service ownership and provider model

The owner's current decision is that Sharm To Go is the accountable public
operator and coordinator for the launch catalog. The customer sees one Sharm
To Go relationship from request through support. Older wording that positioned
the site publicly as a collection of unrelated providers is superseded.

The internal provider/fulfilment model remains useful as operational truth if
Sharm To Go subcontracts any component. It must not expose private commission
or contact data, and it must never weaken Sharm To Go's customer-support
responsibility.

## Simple public model

The customer sees a normal bookable service: description, date, people, pickup,
price, cancellation wording and confirmation type. Provider and commission
details remain internal except for one clear `Operated by` label when the
operator is not Sharm To Go.

## Minimum internal classification

| Field | Allowed meaning |
|---|---|
| Fulfilment model | `DIRECT` or `PARTNER`; avoid `MIXED` at launch |
| Fulfilment owner | The organization legally/operationally delivering the service |
| Request coordinator | Sharm To Go or the provider |
| Capacity authority | The party whose confirmation makes availability valid |
| Price authority | Source, currency, effective time and expiry |
| Customer support owner | Contact path before and after confirmation |
| Cancellation/refund owner | Responsible party and approved policy version |
| Commission agreement | Internal only; never guessed from public price |
| Media/content rights | Source, allowed usage and expiry |
| Publication status | Draft, review, approved, suspended or archived |

The public page defaults to Sharm To Go as the accountable brand. A separate
“operated by” label is shown only when a legally significant third-party
operator identity must be disclosed; it is not the primary marketing message.

## Data needed for each first service

1. Name, category and Arabic/English description.
2. Duration, available dates/times and maximum people.
3. Adult/child or fixed price in EGP and any optional add-ons.
4. Pickup area, inclusions, exclusions and customer preparation.
5. Confirmation type: instant or staff review.
6. Cancellation/refund wording approved for publication.
7. Photos the owner has the right to publish.
8. Provider name only when another business operates the service.

Provider commission, payout and legal documents can be added when partner
settlement is actually implemented. They do not block designing or building the
basic catalog and booking experience.
