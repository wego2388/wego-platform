## Source age and language rules

- Most listings describe children as ages 2–11 or 3–11 and infants as free;
  listing 1 instead uses children 4–11 and infants through age 3.
- Listing 21 flags infants as not permitted even though its general age fields
  also contain an infant range. This conflict must be resolved by the operator.
- Arabic, English, Italian, and Russian are claimed on most source listings.
  Museum listings also claim French; Cairo listings claim a wider set. These
  are research leads only, never guaranteed guide availability.

## Consolidation and verification findings

1. Listings 1, 4, and 12 substantially overlap and should become one city-tour
   service with clean variants, not three competing Sharm To Go cards.
2. Listings 3, 5, 13, 20, and 23 are museum/city combinations and should become
   one museum service with itinerary options.
3. Listings 2, 7, and 24 overlap. The source simultaneously states €870 covers
   up to 10 guests in one listing and up to 20 in two others. Listing 24 says
   “half day” while its duration field says seven hours. Do not choose between
   these values without a yacht operator's written confirmation.
4. Listing 17's €10 child price beside a €120 adult price is unusually large
   variance and requires explicit confirmation.
5. Listing 22's €54.98 base conflicts slightly with its €55 tier. Treat the
   tiers as source evidence, not a price Sharm To Go can publish.
6. Free cancellation is flagged across the source set, and the site commonly
   displays a 12-hour claim. Sharm To Go still needs its own operator-approved
   cancellation/refund wording for every service.
7. Pickup, entrance fees, permits, vehicle/boat capacity, guide language, and
   “instant confirmation” are operational claims. None transfers automatically
   from Egyptra to Sharm To Go.
