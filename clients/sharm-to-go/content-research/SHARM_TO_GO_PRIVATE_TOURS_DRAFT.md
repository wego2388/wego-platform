## Original bilingual copy and candidate variants

### STG-PT-001 — Private Sharm City Essentials

- **English short description:** Discover Sharm's best-known city landmarks at
  a flexible pace, with private transport and time for the Old Market, places
  of worship, viewpoints, and an optional Naama Bay stop.
- **Arabic short description:** اكتشف أشهر معالم مدينة شرم الشيخ بإيقاع مرن،
  مع انتقال خاص ووقت لزيارة السوق القديم ودور العبادة ونقاط المشاهدة، مع
  إمكانية إضافة توقف في خليج نعمة.
- **Candidate duration:** 3 hours.
- **Candidate variants:** standard private tour; female-guide request.
- **Source benchmark:** standard adult €10–€12.50, child €6.50–€8.50;
  female-guide adult €12–€15, child €6–€12. Source schedules span 09:00–20:00.
- **Candidate add-ons:** Farsha transfer, SOHO transfer, museum admission, or a
  meal. Each needs its own operator cost, opening-hours check, and EGP price.

### STG-PT-002 — Sharm Museum and City Choices

- **English short description:** Pair the Sharm El Sheikh Museum with a private
  city itinerary, choosing the Old Market, Farsha, SOHO Square, or a broader
  highlights route according to the selected package.
- **Arabic short description:** اجمع بين زيارة متحف شرم الشيخ وجولة خاصة داخل
  المدينة، مع اختيار السوق القديم أو فرشة أو سوهو سكوير أو مسار أشمل لأهم
  المعالم حسب الباقة.
- **Candidate duration:** 3–5 hours, depending on the route.
- **Candidate variants:** museum and Old Market; museum and Farsha; museum and
  SOHO; museum with full city highlights.
- **Source benchmark:** adults €18–€25, children €8–€15; some source pages use a
  higher solo-traveller tier. Observed start times range from 09:30 to 20:00.
- **Critical check:** museum ticket type, opening hours, guide licensing, and
  whether drinks/table reservations at Farsha are excluded.

### STG-PT-003 — Sharm City and Parasailing

- **English short description:** Combine a private introduction to Sharm with
  a short parasailing flight above the Red Sea, subject to weather and the
  marine operator's safety decision.
- **Arabic short description:** اجمع بين جولة خاصة للتعرف على شرم الشيخ وتجربة
  باراسيلنج قصيرة فوق البحر الأحمر، وفق حالة الطقس وقرار مشغّل النشاط البحري.
- **Candidate duration:** 4 hours.
- **Candidate variant:** tandem parasailing; solo flight only as an approved
  upgrade.
- **Source benchmark:** adult €39, child €34, solo upgrade €10; 09:00–11:00.
- **Critical check:** minimum age/weight, maximum combined weight, weather
  cancellation, insurance, equipment, and whether photos/video cost extra.

### STG-PT-004 — City Sights and Desert Adventure

- **English short description:** Split one private outing between Sharm's city
  highlights and an ATV ride into the nearby desert, with optional camel ride,
  dinner, and stargazing components.
- **Arabic short description:** اقضِ جولة خاصة تجمع بين معالم شرم الشيخ وقيادة
  الدراجة الرباعية في الصحراء القريبة، مع خيارات لركوب الجمل والعشاء ومشاهدة
  النجوم.
- **Candidate duration:** 5 hours before optional extensions.
- **Source benchmark:** city/ATV adult €23 and child €15; dinner/camel version
  €36/€23; telescope stargazing €8. Observed starts: 10:00 or 14:00.
- **Critical check:** ATV driver/passenger ages, helmet/goggle inclusion,
  route permit, meal venue, show details, and return time.

### STG-PT-007 — Private Car and Driver in Sharm

- **English short description:** Reserve a private vehicle and driver for up to
  three agreed stops inside Sharm, with the route confirmed before pickup.
- **Arabic short description:** احجز سيارة خاصة بسائق لزيارة ما يصل إلى ثلاث
  محطات متفق عليها داخل شرم الشيخ، مع تأكيد المسار قبل موعد الاستلام.
- **Candidate duration:** 3 hours.
- **Recommended price basis:** `PER_VEHICLE`, subject to vehicle class and
  passenger capacity; the source instead presents per-adult tiers.
- **Source benchmark:** €24.90 for one person or €11.50 per adult for 2–13,

- **Critical check:** vehicle class, licensed passenger capacity, kilometre and

## Mandatory completion checklist for every service

Before creating a publishable catalog record, replace every unknown with
operator-approved evidence:

1. Legal operator/provider name and internal operational contact.
2. Exact itinerary, duration, operating days, start times, and blackout dates.
3. Maximum participants and licensed vehicle/boat capacity.
4. Final adult/child/group EGP price, price basis, tax/fee treatment, effective
   date, and expiry date.
5. Child and infant ages plus activity-specific health, mobility, weight, and
   accessibility restrictions.
6. Pickup areas, supplements, meeting point, pickup window, and late policy.
7. Final inclusions, exclusions, equipment, meals, entry fees, and add-ons.
8. Confirmation SLA, cancellation/refund wording, bad-weather alternative, and
   no-show policy approved for Sharm To Go.
9. Customer-support owner and escalation path before/during/after the service.
10. Original or licensed media with rights evidence; no Egyptra image may be
    attached merely because its public URL is accessible.
11. Final Arabic and English editorial review and commercial approval.

Until all eleven items are complete, the service remains a private research
draft and must not advance to `REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, or `PUBLISHED`.
