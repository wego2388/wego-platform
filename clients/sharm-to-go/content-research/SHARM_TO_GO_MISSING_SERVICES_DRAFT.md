## Original bilingual copy and candidate variants

### STG-SEA-001 — Ras Mohammed & White Island Snorkeling Cruise

- **English short description:** Spend a full day on the Red Sea with a shared
  boat, guided snorkeling stops, time near White Island, and an onboard meal,
  subject to park access and sea conditions.
- **Arabic short description:** اقضِ يومًا كاملًا في البحر الأحمر على متن قارب
  مشترك، مع محطات سنوركلينج بصحبة مرشد ووقت بالقرب من الجزيرة البيضاء ووجبة
  على القارب، وفق تصاريح المحمية وحالة البحر.
- **Candidate duration:** 7 hours; source start 09:00.
- **Candidate variants:** equipment excluded; equipment included. A premium
  vessel/meal should be a separate option only when the actual vessel and
  operator are the same commercial record.
- **Source benchmark:** adult €28/child €17 without gear; €29.90/€18 with gear;
  source lists a mandatory €5 park fee.
- **Critical check:** named boat and marina, licensed capacity, passenger
  manifest, park permit/fee, guide and life-jacket provision, swimming ability,
  exact island-access wording, meal/allergies, pickup, and weather cancellation.
- **Boundary:** do not attach intro-dive or certified-dive add-ons in Sharm To
  Go; route those through the Sharm Divers Club ownership decision.

## Recommended launch order

The lowest-complexity first interview target is `STG-TRN-001` because its scope
can be made precise without marine, animal, or attraction inventory. It is not
automatically approved: a licensed transfer provider still has to supply every
required fact and an EGP price.

After that, a sensible evidence-first order is:

1. `STG-SEA-003` or `STG-NAT-001`, after vessel/park evidence.
2. `STG-FAM-001`, after a direct venue ticket agreement.
3. Sea cruises and desert services after permits, safety, capacity, and weather
   policies are documented.
4. Long-distance, flight, cross-border, and overnight services only after the
   simpler local operating workflow is proven end to end.
