## Coverage decision

The source repeats small wording, transport, meal, and equipment variations as
separate cards. Sharm To Go should start with one understandable service concept
per genuine operating pattern, then expose confirmed differences as options.
The final operator evidence may still require a concept to be split when routes,
vessels, licences, cancellation rules, or providers are materially different.

| Source family | Sharm To Go treatment |
|---|---|
| Private city, museum, yacht, speedboat, ATV, Dahab, and Cairo products | Already represented by `STG-PT-001` through `STG-PT-014`; do not duplicate. |
| Ras Mohammed/White Island group cruises | Add one cruise concept with equipment and service-level options. |
| Tiran group cruises | Add separately because route, permits, and marine conditions differ. |
| Glass-bottom boat, parasailing, and combined water sports | Add two simple concepts plus one optional cross-terrain combo. |
| Repeated quad/buggy/camel/dinner/stargazing cards | Consolidate into five operationally distinct desert concepts. |
| Ras Mohammed by road and group Dahab/canyon trips | Add two land/nature concepts; do not mix them with boat cruises or the existing private Dahab concept. |
| Mount Sinai, St Catherine, Cairo, Luxor, Petra, and overnight trips | Add as heritage/day-trip concepts, with long-distance and overnight products kept in a later rollout wave. |
| Aqua park | Add as a family concept after venue and ticket evidence. |
| Dolphin show/swim | Record as a hold, not a catalog concept, pending welfare, venue, safety, and legal review. |
| Airport and named-venue transfers | Add a dedicated airport concept and one generic venue-return concept. |
| Introductory/certified diving and PADI training | Do not duplicate in Sharm To Go; route product ownership to Sharm Divers Club first. |

The current source set contains no useful evidence for the proposed `Wellness
and leisure` category. That remains a real research gap; no spa, hammam, beach
day, or similar service has been invented to make the catalog appear complete.

## Source-value boundary

All source prices are EUR comparison snapshots on the check date. They are not
Sharm To Go costs or sell prices, and no exchange-rate conversion is allowed.
Source schedules, inclusions, fees, ages, languages, capacities, cancellation
claims, and availability remain unverified until a real operator supplies
written facts for Sharm To Go in EGP.
