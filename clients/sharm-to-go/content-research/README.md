# Sharm To Go content research

This directory contains text-only commercial research. It is not launch
inventory and must never be seeded, published, or presented as a Sharm To Go
offer without completing the repository's service-content approval process.

## Current research set

- [`EGYPTRA_PRIVATE_TOURS_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md`](EGYPTRA_PRIVATE_TOURS_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md)
  records the 24 Sharm El Sheikh private-tour results visible on the referenced
  source page on 2026-09-03. It keeps source prices and options for comparison,
  while deliberately excluding images, reviews, ratings, long descriptions,
  and supplier marketing claims.
- [`SHARM_TO_GO_PRIVATE_TOURS_DRAFT.md`](SHARM_TO_GO_PRIVATE_TOURS_DRAFT.md)
  consolidates those overlapping results into 14 clearer Sharm To Go service
  concepts with original Arabic and English copy.
- [`EGYPTRA_SHARM_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`](EGYPTRA_SHARM_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md)
  compares all five pages of the broader Sharm listing: 123 returned cards,
  122 unique source slugs, category counts, family-level coverage decisions,
  representative evidence, and the deliberate diving/dolphin holds.
- [`SHARM_TO_GO_MISSING_SERVICES_DRAFT.md`](SHARM_TO_GO_MISSING_SERVICES_DRAFT.md)
  adds 23 non-duplicate, bilingual concepts for sea, desert, nature, family,
  transfer, heritage, long-distance, and overnight services. It also gives a
  risk-based rollout order and original short copy for every concept.
- [`SHARM_TO_GO_SERVICE_INTAKE_SHEETS.md`](SHARM_TO_GO_SERVICE_INTAKE_SHEETS.md)
  reformats all 37 concepts, field-for-field, into the exact structure
  `design/SERVICE_CONTENT_TEMPLATE.md` requires before publication. Every
  field this research can responsibly fill in already is; every field only
  a real operator/owner decision can supply (identity, EGP price, capacity,
  schedule, cancellation wording, media rights) is marked `TO CONFIRM`, not
  guessed. This is the fastest real path from research to Packet 1E — fill
  one block's `TO CONFIRM` lines with real facts and it becomes the actual
  input for creating that service through the real staff workflow.

The research catalog therefore contains **37 original, non-duplicate Sharm To
Go concepts**: 14 from the private-tour subset and 23 from the broader gap
audit. That count is not a claim that 37 real services or providers exist.

## Publication boundary

Every record in this directory is `RESEARCH_ONLY`. Source-site prices are EUR
snapshots belonging to another operator; they are not Sharm To Go prices and
must not be converted mechanically. A real Sharm To Go service remains blocked
until its actual operator confirms an EGP price and validity period, capacity,
pickup coverage, availability, inclusions/exclusions, safety restrictions,
cancellation wording, support ownership, and media rights.

No image URL or asset from the source site has been copied.
